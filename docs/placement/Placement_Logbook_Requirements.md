# Placement Logbook Requirements

## Overview

The placement logbook records your placement activity from the **start to the mid-point**. It serves two purposes:
1. Record what you did on placement
2. Reflect on how you developed your skills using the **Borton Model of Reflection**

At the mid-point, both your logbook and supervisor review will be assessed by the unit team.

---

## Structure

Your logbook must contain **five columns**:

| Column | Name | Description |
|--------|------|-------------|
| 1 | **Date** | Date of the placement activity |
| 2 | **Time Spent** | How long you spent on the activity |
| 3 | **Activity Description** | The WHAT stage of reflection |
| 4 | **Supervisor Feedback** | The SO WHAT stage of reflection |
| 5 | **Reflection** | The NOW WHAT stage of reflection |

> One row per day on placement is recommended so you don't forget what you completed.

---

## Borton Model of Reflection

Each row maps to the three stages of the Borton Model:

### WHAT (Column 3 — Activity Description)
- Describe what you did in enough detail for someone **external to the organisation** to understand
- Example: *"Today I organised and attended a client meeting for my placement project. I had to email the client to organise the meeting. This activity shows that I have communicated in a professional manner during my placement."*

### SO WHAT (Column 4 — Supervisor Feedback)
- Record feedback received from your supervisor or team members **in your own words**
- Include suggestions for improvement and any actions you took in response
- Example: *"My supervisor sent me a message after the meeting with the client, stating that I did a good job. This shows that I can conduct myself professionally, ensuring I maintain respect for myself and my colleagues through professional language and behaviour."*

### NOW WHAT (Column 5 — Reflection)
- Reflect on what you learned from the activity and feedback
- **Link explicitly to the supervisor/performance review criteria**
- Describe how this skill development will help you in the future
- Example: *"Now that I know I can contribute professionally, I can extend my communication to further professional contexts while also maintaining timely and respectful interactions with all stakeholders."*

---

## Evidencing the Supervisor/Performance Review Criteria

Your logbook reflections must serve as evidence that you have met each rubric criterion.

To do this:
- **Name specific skills** (e.g. communication, critical thinking, initiative, time management)
- **Describe how you demonstrated that skill** during a placement activity
- **Explain how the skill will help you beyond placement** (future impact)
- Cover **all rubric criteria** to at least a satisfactory level by the end of placement

> The supervisor's performance rating does not directly affect your grade, but you must address all criteria in your logbook to pass the placement hurdle.

---

## Passing Requirements

- [ ] Logbook submitted from start of placement to the mid-point
- [ ] Five-column structure used throughout
- [ ] Each entry uses WHAT / SO WHAT / NOW WHAT structure
- [ ] Reflections link to supervisor/performance review rubric criteria
- [ ] All rubric criteria addressed to at least satisfactory level by end of placement
- [ ] Supervisor review completed at mid-point
